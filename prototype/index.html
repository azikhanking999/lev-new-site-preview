        Questions

        Good to know.

        What is the minimum order?

        Six pieces. You can mix sizes, and add more colours of the same item to one order.

        How do you decorate?

        Full-colour printing (DTF) and embroidery. For large runs in one or two colours we also screen print: call us for a quote.

        Do you check my design?

        Yes. A person on our art team looks at every design before it goes to production and contacts you if something looks off.

        How fast can I get it?

        Standard delivery is free and the estimate is shown on each product page. If you have a deadline, call (305) 705-5486 and we will tell you what is possible.

        Can I order for a whole team with names and numbers?

        Yes. In the design studio, open Names, type your list once, and each piece gets its own name, number or both.

        What if I only have a rough idea?

        Start in the studio with text and clipart, or send us what you have. We will help you get it print-ready.

        Rather talk it through?

        Real people, in Miami.

        Tell us what you are making and when you need it. We will suggest the item, the method and the price.
