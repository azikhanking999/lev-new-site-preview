    How we decorate it

    We print this item with full-colour transfers (DTF). Use as many colours as you like: the price depends on the size of the print and how many places you print, not on colours.

    Ordering a large run in one or two colours? Ask us about screen printing: (305) 705-5486.

    Questions

    What is the minimum order?

    Six pieces for this item. You can mix sizes freely, and add more colours of the same shirt to one order.

    How does the bulk discount work?

    The price per piece drops as the quantity goes up: at 12, 24, 48, 72, 144 and 301 pieces. The discount covers both the shirt and the print. The calculator above shows the exact price.

    Can I upload my own design?

    Yes. Open the design studio and upload a PNG, JPG or SVG, or build a design there with text and art. A person on our team checks every design before it prints.

    When will it arrive?

    Standard delivery is free. The date shown near the top of this page is the current estimate. If you have a deadline, call us and we will tell you what is possible.
